How moving prices work

Every move is different. These are some of the most common factors that can affect the time, crew requirements, and final cost of a moving job.

⏱️

Time affects labor cost

Home size, packing level, stairs, elevator wait times, walking distance, furniture, and organization can all affect how long a move takes.

📏

Distance and access matter

Mileage, parking location, loading access, and long carries can affect the amount of time and travel involved in the move.

🏋️

Heavy items change the job

Pianos, safes, appliances, marble pieces, oversized furniture, and certain gym equipment may require additional planning, equipment, or labor.

Before you request an estimate

Know your move type

Check access points

List specialty items

Flag anything oversized, fragile, unusually heavy, or difficult to move so the crew can plan appropriately.

Frequently asked moving questions

Understanding a few common moving terms can make planning easier and help you provide better information when requesting an estimate.

How do I know how many movers I need?

Crew size depends on the amount being moved, access conditions, furniture size, and the desired timeline. Movers4Hire can review the details before confirming the crew.

What is a long carry?

A long carry generally means the moving truck cannot park close to the entrance, requiring the crew to carry items a longer distance.

What counts as a specialty item?

Safes, pianos, large appliances, marble pieces, oversized furniture, and certain exercise equipment are common examples.

Does packing affect the estimate?

It can. A fully packed home may take less moving time than a home where loose items still need to be boxed, wrapped, or prepared.

Why does access matter?

Stairs, elevators, loading zones, parking restrictions, narrow hallways, and walking distance can all affect the time and difficulty of a move.

Can photos help with my estimate?

Yes. Photos can provide useful context about room fullness, furniture size, boxes, access, and specialty items before the moving team reviews your request.
