How moving prices actually work

Most customers have not moved in years, so this section explains the basics in plain English and helps them know what to ask before they request a quote.

⏱️

Time affects labor cost

Hourly pricing depends on home size, access, organization, stairs, elevator wait times, and how prepared everything is before the crew arrives.

📏

Distance and carry matter

Local jobs may include mileage, truck fees, or long-carry charges when movers must walk a significant distance from the truck to the home.

🏋️

Heavy items change the job

Pianos, safes, appliances, marble tops, and oversized furniture often require extra time, more labor, special tools, or added protection.

Common questions people forget to ask

This helps customers who have not moved in a long time understand what matters before move day, what can affect price, and what info helps create a better quote.

How do I know how many movers I need?

Studios and small apartments often work with 2 movers. Bigger homes, stairs, large furniture, and tighter timelines usually benefit from 3 or 4.

What is a long carry?

A long carry means the truck cannot park close to the entrance, so movers spend extra time walking items a longer distance.

What counts as a specialty item?

Safes, pianos, large appliances, marble pieces, oversized sectionals, and certain gym equipment can require extra labor or equipment.

Does packing affect the quote?

Yes. Fully packed homes usually move faster than homes where loose items still need to be boxed, wrapped, or protected by the crew.

Why does access matter?

Flights of stairs, elevators, reserved loading zones, parking limits, and narrow hallways can all change time and difficulty.

Can photos help with estimates?

Yes. Photo uploads can make quotes more informed by showing room fullness, visible boxes, furniture size, and special handling needs.
